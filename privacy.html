Privacy

Simple, first-party
analytics.

This portfolio uses a small first-party analytics service to understand which pages and projects are useful, how visitors find the site, and which contact or project links are used.

What is collected
  • Pseudonymous random browser and session identifiers, page and project views, and intentional link or screenshot interactions.
  • Approved UTM campaign values, referring domain only, approximate device/browser/operating-system category, and country-level location supplied by Cloudflare.
What is not collected
  • No raw IP address, precise location, full referrer URL, form content, clipboard content, password, or invasive browser fingerprint.
  • No cross-site advertising profile or sale of analytics data.
01 Your choices

Control on this browser.

Raw event records are retained for approximately 180 days, then removed by scheduled cleanup. Aggregated daily totals may remain without individual event records. Random identifiers estimate browsers, not unique people.

To stop future analytics from this browser, run localStorage.setItem('portfolio_ignore', '1') in the browser console while on this site. To re-enable it, run localStorage.removeItem('portfolio_ignore'). Where opt-in consent is required, tracking remains off until it is granted through the site prompt.

Referrer attribution is inferred when a browser provides it. UTM-tagged links are explicit campaign information and are more dependable. Missing referrers are reported as unknown or unattributed, not confirmed direct visits.
